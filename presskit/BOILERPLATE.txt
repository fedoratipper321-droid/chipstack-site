Long (boilerplate):
Chipstack Studios is an independent game studio run by a single person,
Mikael, out of the Aland Islands. Founded in February 2026, the studio
builds tactile, opinionated mobile games designed for short sessions and
long obsessions. Its first two titles, Blackjack Empire (September 2026)
and Salvage Junkies (October 2026), are available on Google Play.

Salvage Junkies (short):
A tactile salvage game set in Lowtide, a strange coastal town where junk
washes ashore and nobody remembers who built it. Take wrecks apart on the
bench, piece by piece, sort the materials, and climb from toasters to
orbital debris.

Blackjack Empire (short):
A blackjack game that escalates into a casino empire simulator. Win hands,
buy casinos, cover payroll, trade markets, climb VIP ranks, and end up
running a casino in orbit. Simulated gambling for adults 18+, virtual
currency only, no real-money winnings.
