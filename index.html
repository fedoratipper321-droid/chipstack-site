ABOUT

Chipstack Studios is one person making games in the Åland Islands.

That person is Mikael, and "we" means him, a desk, and whatever he decides to make next. He started in web development and wanted a bigger creative space: somewhere he could build anything he could think of. Games turned out to be that space.

We like games with strong opinions: tactile little machines that are easy to pick up and hard to put down. No suits, no committees, no four-hundred-person teams. Just a stubborn one-person studio stacking one strange idea on top of another and seeing what holds.

Everything we make is built mobile-first, designed for short sessions and long obsessions, and finished with more polish than our roadmap probably allows.
