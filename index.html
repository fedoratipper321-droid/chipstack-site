ABOUT

Chipstack Studios is an independent game studio making distinctive, experimental, accessible games.

We like games with strong opinions: tactile little machines that are easy to pick up and hard to put down. No suits, no committees, no four-hundred-person teams. Just a stubborn small studio stacking one strange idea on top of another and seeing what holds.

Everything we make is built mobile-first, designed for short sessions and long obsessions, and finished with more polish than our roadmap probably allows.
