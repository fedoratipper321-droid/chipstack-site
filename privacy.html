THE SHORT VERSION

We're a small game studio, not a data company. This website collects no personal data, sets no tracking cookies, and runs no analytics. Our games are designed to respect your time and your privacy.

THIS WEBSITE

The Chipstack Studios website is a static site. It does not use cookies, tracking pixels, analytics services, or fingerprinting of any kind. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts; Google's own privacy policy applies to that request.
