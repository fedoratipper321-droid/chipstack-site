SLICEBOX

PRIVACY POLICY

SLICEBOX · LAST UPDATED NOVEMBER 2026

THE SHORT VERSION

The game itself collects nothing. Everything it stores stays on your device. It shows ads served by Google AdMob and offers optional purchases through Google Play, and those services process some data under their own policies.

WHAT WE COLLECT OURSELVES

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no servers run by us that receive your data.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Save data. Your game progress, scores, coins, unlocks and settings are stored in the app's local storage on your device. They never leave your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Purchase records. Whether you own ad removal is stored locally so the game knows not to show you ads. The transaction itself is handled by Google Play.

ADVERTISING (GOOGLE ADMOB)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, including rewarded ads you can choose to watch for in-game benefits. To serve ads, Google may process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information, and ad interactions. This data is processed by Google under Google's own policies; we do not see or store it.

Ads personalization is governed by your device settings and, where required (EEA/UK), by a consent prompt shown when the app first runs. You can reset or disable your advertising ID in your Android settings. An optional in-app purchase removes ads entirely.

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

IN-APP PURCHASES

Optional purchases — such as removing ads or buying coin packs — are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details; Google handles the entire transaction. The app only receives confirmation that a purchase succeeded so it can grant the item.

PERMISSIONS AND WHY THEY EXIST

Internet (INTERNET). Required to load ads and verify purchases.

Vibration. Haptic feedback only: it carries gameplay feedback, not data.

CHILDREN

Slicebox is designed to be enjoyed by players of all ages. The app itself collects no personal information from anyone, including children, and there are no social features or chat. Ads are served by Google AdMob; ads shown to users identified as children are subject to Google's protections for children.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
