LONG

Chipstack Studios is an independent game studio run by a single person, Mikael, out of the Åland Islands. Founded in February 2026, the studio builds tactile, opinionated mobile games designed for short sessions and long obsessions. Its first two titles, Blackjack Empire (September 2026) and Salvage Junkies (October 2026), are available on Google Play.

SALVAGE JUNKIES

A tactile salvage game set in Lowtide, a strange coastal town where junk washes ashore and nobody remembers who built it. Take wrecks apart on the bench, piece by piece, and climb from toasters to orbital debris. Released October 2026 on Google Play.

BLACKJACK EMPIRE

A blackjack game that escalates into a casino empire simulator. Win hands, buy casinos, cover payroll, trade markets, climb VIP ranks, and end up running a casino in orbit. Simulated gambling for adults 18+, virtual currency only, no real-money winnings. Released September 2026 on Google Play.
